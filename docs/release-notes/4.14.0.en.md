This update makes FewohBee more international and more versatile: your guests book and check in in their own language, the community is translating the app into further languages – Greek is already on board – and you can now simply rent out your seminar room or banquet hall by the day. On top of that, a surcharge per additional person means a single room price covers every occupancy. Many of the big and small improvements come from @MeisterAdebar – thank you very much! Enjoy trying it all out.

## 🌍 Your guests in their own language

Whether they come from Paris, Prague or Passau: your guests now book, check in and read their emails in their own language. You decide which languages you offer and only translate the texts that matter to you.

* **Choose languages:** Under **Settings → General → Guests** you decide in which languages your online booking and your online check-in appear.
* **Translate your texts:** Wherever guests read something – room categories, extras, tourist tax, arrival notice or the texts of online booking and online check-in – you add the translation directly below the field. If one is missing, the text in your installation's language simply steps in.
* **The booking page speaks along:** When guests open it directly, it follows their browser language and offers a language selector. If it is embedded in your website, you set the language via the `lang` parameter in the iFrame address, e.g. `…/book?embed=1&lang=en`. Amounts appear in the format the guest is used to, and the preselected country matches their browser.
* **Emails and PDFs in the guest's language:** Create language variants of your templates in the template list. Automatic emails to the booker, including attached PDFs such as terms and conditions or invoices, then arrive in their language, otherwise with the template itself as usual. FewohBee remembers the language from online booking and online check-in, and you can change it on the guest at any time. Notifications to you stay in your installation's language.
* **Salutations per language:** You now maintain salutations as a table with their version in each language. The salutation saved on the guest is still the one in your installation's language.

## 🤝 FewohBee is learning new languages – with your help

Until now, FewohBee was available in German and English. From now on, the community can translate the app into further languages directly in the browser on [Weblate](https://hosted.weblate.org/projects/fewohbee/), no programming skills needed. **Greek leads the way** and is available as the first community language with this update ([#251](https://github.com/developeregrem/fewohbee/issues/251)). A heartfelt thank you to everyone who helped make it happen!

* A language becomes selectable once the most important areas are translated; anything still missing appears in English in the meantime.
* Do you speak French, Italian or Polish? Join in – every translated line helps. How to get started is described in the [wiki](https://github.com/developeregrem/fewohbee/wiki/Translations).

## 🏛️ Event spaces

You can now rent out your seminar room, banquet hall, barn or outdoor area properly: booked by the day, with their own prices and kept cleanly separate from your overnight stays. The party that used to be booked as an overnight stay from Saturday to Sunday is a thing of the past.

📖 [Room types in the documentation](https://www.fewohbee.app/documentation/manual-types.html)

* **Finally bookable by the day:** Under **Settings → Room categories**, choose the room type "Event space" for a category. "To" is then the last day – "Sat to Sat" is exactly one day. In the occupancy plan the event fills whole days, and both reservation and invoice refer to days instead of nights.
* **One price for the whole group:** Event spaces have the calculation types "Per day, per room" and "Per day, per person". Together with the new surcharge per additional person, a single price is enough: €500 for 50 people plus a €5 surcharge make €550 for 60 people. Each price applies either to accommodation or to event spaces.
* **Honest figures, no tourist tax:** The guests at the wedding party no longer count as overnight stays. Event spaces are left out of occupancy, overnight and origin statistics, so they no longer distort the occupancy rules of your dynamic pricing either. No tourist tax is charged for them, and of course their revenue still counts.
* **Everything in its right place:** Event spaces do not show up in online booking, and reservations can only be moved to rooms of the same room type. That way the party does not end up in the holiday apartment – and the holiday guest does not end up in the seminar room.
* **For your own templates and interfaces:** Internally, an event still ends on the following day, so "Sat to Sat" is stored as "Sat to Sun". The REST API, the AI interface and `endDate` in templates provide this following day; for the last day, use `visibleEndDate` in templates (for overnight stays that is the departure day).
* **What is not possible (yet):** Booking is in whole days, not by the hour or half day, and event spaces cannot be booked online yet.

## 💶 Surcharge per additional person

One room, four possible occupancies, four prices – and if you forgot one, you were in trouble. Not any more: a single room price with a surcharge per additional person covers every occupancy.

* **How it works:** For prices of type "Per night, per room", you enter a "Surcharge per additional person" in the "Room specific" section. Example: €80 for 2 people and a €15 surcharge make €110 for 4 people.
* **"Up to" instead of "exactly":** A price with a surcharge applies to **up to** its number of people; only above that does each additional person, up to the number of beds, cost the surcharge per night. Anyone travelling alone pays €80 in the example. If there is a price for exactly the booked number of people, such as a cheaper single price, it takes precedence; prices without a surcharge still apply only to exactly their number of people. If a room should only be bookable online from a minimum number of guests, "Min. occupancy" under **Settings → Online booking** helps you.
* **Included everywhere:** The surcharge appears under "Surcharges / discounts" on the reservation, the invoice and in online booking. Price rules and day prices adjust it in the same proportion as the room price, and the price calendar, REST API and AI interface take it into account too.

## 🗓️ More flexible calendar export

If you share your occupancy via an iCal calendar, you now have more say in what ends up there.

* **Arrival and departure times:** On request, the export includes saved arrival and departure times – switch this on in the room's export settings. If one of the two times is missing, it is filled in as 00:00; without times, and for room blocks, entries stay all-day.
* **Only what matters:** By default, only current and future reservations and room blocks end up in the calendar, stays in progress included. If you need the past as well, choose **"Entire calendar"**.
* **Tidier settings:** Export options and calendar addresses are now neatly arranged, and you copy addresses with one click.

## ✉️ Emails and invoices

Fewer clicks to a finished email – and invoices that reach the right recipient straight away.

* **Booking confirmation with one click:** The new "Confirmation" button is waiting for you in the reservation details. It opens the email directly for editing – rooms, recipient and PDF attachments such as confirmation and terms and conditions are already filled in. You choose the email template and attachments under **Settings → General → Booking confirmation**; without an email template the button stays hidden. And when you are in a hurry, "Send right away" sends the email without a detour. Contribution by @MeisterAdebar ([#318](https://github.com/developeregrem/fewohbee/discussions/318)).
* **Send the online check-in invitation manually:** If you have chosen an "Invitation template" under **Settings → Online check-in**, you send the invitation directly from the reservation's "Online check-in" tab – even a second time if it got lost. The status line tells you whether it has already gone out or whether an automation takes care of it. Contribution by @MeisterAdebar ([#318](https://github.com/developeregrem/fewohbee/discussions/318)).
* **Company details right at check-in:** Business travellers can enter company, VAT ID and a different invoice address in the online check-in; you switch this on under **Settings → Online check-in**. On confirmation, the details end up in a business address of the main guest and therefore straight on their invoices.
* **A separate email for invoices:** Should the invoice go to the accounting department instead of the guest? Simply save an "Email for invoices" on the contact address. New invoices are then sent there; existing ones remain unchanged. If you attach an invoice to an email from the reservation, the recipient switches along with it, and a note lets you know. Contribution by @MeisterAdebar ([#312](https://github.com/developeregrem/fewohbee/issues/312)).
* **Embedded templates:** Your signature and other templates of type "General Email Template" can now be embedded in all email templates, and in language variants automatically in the matching language. Also fixed: if a template was saved again in the visual editor or inserted by dragging, the link was lost, and emails and PDFs showed only the template's name (see "When upgrading").

## ✨ Further improvements

Lots of small things that make your day-to-day work more pleasant:

* **Room selection in online booking:** In the "Modern" design, guests conveniently choose the quantity per room and occupancy with "+" and "−". If only one selection fits the search, it is already filled in; otherwise a note above "Continue" tells them what is still missing.
* **Sorting by name:** Prices and extras are now sorted by name everywhere, always in the same order – no more searching.
* **Create calendar entries faster:** From the header of the reservation overview, the form now only asks for calendar, title and time; you set a different date or several days via the link below. Contribution by @MeisterAdebar ([#316](https://github.com/developeregrem/fewohbee/issues/316)).
* **Tidier popover:** Phone numbers and email addresses in the popover of the reservation bars no longer wrap mid-line. Invoice with status (one click opens it), contact details and times each have their own neat line with an icon. Contribution by @MeisterAdebar ([#314](https://github.com/developeregrem/fewohbee/issues/314)).
* **Holidays stay selected:** The federal state chosen under **Settings → Display settings for holidays** is now kept in the reservation overview even after reloading. Contribution by @MeisterAdebar ([#315](https://github.com/developeregrem/fewohbee/issues/315)).
* **Paging in the booking journal:** With the arrows at the edge of the screen you page through pages and months, and in the overview and year view through the years – on tablets and smartphones with a swipe too. The arrows next to the month name are removed in return. "Add booking" is the round "+" button at the bottom right on touch devices. Contribution by @MeisterAdebar.
* **Smartphone polish:** Wide tables scroll sideways on their own instead of making the whole page wider. The buttons in the booking journal and for custom automations stay on screen, "New automation" is a round "+" button on touch devices, and month and year selection is back in the tourism statistics. Contribution by @MeisterAdebar ([#327](https://github.com/developeregrem/fewohbee/issues/327)).
* **Under the bonnet:** Numerous internal clean-ups, bug fixes and additional automated tests make FewohBee more robust – for everything still to come as well.

## 🐞 Bug fixes

Thank you to everyone who reported bugs – it helps enormously!

* **Calendar import secured:** Until now, a calendar address could also be used to query internal services on the server or in the network. Preview and automatic imports now block such requests by default.
* **Calendar import across the clock change:** All-day entries spanning the clock change end on the correct day again. Previously the last night was missing in spring, and conflicts arose in autumn. The next import corrects existing reservations as long as the entry is still in the feed. Contribution by @MeisterAdebar ([#318](https://github.com/developeregrem/fewohbee/discussions/318#discussioncomment-19265442)).
* **Online booking with a percentage discount:** If a discounted price fell exactly on half a cent (e.g. 25% off €44.50), the booking reported a changed price on every attempt – and the guest could not complete it.
* **Online check-in with ID requirement:** If another guest was entered or the prefilled name was overwritten, the form went through even without an ID number.
* **Calendar export with the same arrival and departure day:** Some calendars rejected such reservations. They now occupy the whole day.
* **Check receipts:** Receipt proposals could not be discarded, and after a booking of €0.00 or a negative amount, receipts from the same period could no longer be opened.
* **AI assistants:** Under **Settings → AI assistants**, accesses could not be revoked via the bin icon.

## ⚠️ When upgrading

Three quick points, and you are good to go:

* **Check calendar imports:** If your self-hosted installation imports calendars from sources in the local network, you can allow them specifically – the instructions are in the [wiki](https://github.com/developeregrem/fewohbee/wiki/Konfiguration#importing-calendars-from-the-local-network).
* **Check calendar export:** After the update, existing calendars also export only current and future entries. If past entries should still be transferred, choose **"Entire calendar"** in the room's export settings.
* **Check embedded templates:** Take a quick look at your existing templates using the preview. If only the name of an embedded template appears in its place, delete the entry and insert it again.
