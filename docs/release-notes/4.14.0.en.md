Guests from abroad now book and check in in their own language, seminar rooms and banquet halls can be rented by the day, and a single room price covers every occupancy thanks to a surcharge per additional person. There are also many everyday improvements, a large share of them contributed by @MeisterAdebar. Thank you!

## 🌍 Online booking and online check-in in several languages

Your guests book, check in and read their emails in their own language – you only translate the texts that matter to you.

* **Set languages:** Under **Settings → General → Guests** you choose the languages in which online booking and online check-in are offered.
* **Translate your own texts:** Under the fields guests see, you add translations, for example for room categories, extras, tourist tax, arrival notice and the texts of online booking and online check-in. If a translation is missing, the text appears in the installation's language.
* **Booking page:** When opened directly, it follows the browser language and offers a language selector. When embedded, your website sets the language via the `lang` parameter in the iFrame address, e.g. `…/book?embed=1&lang=en`. Amounts are shown in the format of the guest's language, and the preselected country follows the browser.
* **Emails and PDFs in the guest's language:** Templates get language variants in the template list. Automatic emails to the booker, including attached PDFs such as terms and conditions or invoices, use the variant in their language, otherwise the template itself. FewohBee remembers the language from online booking and online check-in, and you can change it on the guest. Notifications to you stay in the installation's language.
* **Salutations per language:** You maintain salutations as a table with their version in each language. The salutation saved on the guest is the one in the installation's language.
* **More languages from the community:** Besides German and English, the community can now translate FewohBee into further languages directly in the browser on [Weblate](https://hosted.weblate.org/projects/fewohbee/). A language becomes selectable once the most important areas are translated; missing texts appear in English. Help is very welcome – how to get involved is described in the [wiki](https://github.com/developeregrem/fewohbee/wiki/Translations).

## 🏛️ Event spaces

Seminar rooms, banquet halls, barns or outdoor areas can now be rented out properly: booked by the day, with their own prices and kept cleanly separate from overnight stays.

* **Finally bookable by the day:** Under **Settings → Room categories** you choose the room type "Event space" for a category. "To" is then the last day, so "From Sat to Sat" is exactly one day. In the occupancy plan the event fills whole days, and reservation and invoice refer to days instead of nights.
* **One price for the whole group:** Here the calculation types are called "Per day, per room" and "Per day, per person". With the new surcharge per additional person, a single price is enough: €500 for 50 people and a €5 surcharge make €550 for 60 people. A price applies either to accommodation or to event spaces.
* **Clean statistics, no tourist tax:** Event spaces are left out of occupancy, overnight and origin statistics, so they no longer distort the occupancy rules of dynamic pricing either. No tourist tax is charged for them, and their revenue still counts.
* **Nothing ends up in the wrong place:** Event spaces do not appear in online booking, and a reservation can only be moved to a room of the same room type. This way the party does not end up in the holiday apartment and the holiday guest does not end up in the seminar room.
* **For your own templates and interfaces:** Internally, an event still ends on the following day, so "Sat to Sat" is stored as "Sat to Sun". The REST API, the AI interface and `endDate` in templates provide this following day; for the last day, `visibleEndDate` is available in templates (the departure day for overnight stays).
* **Limits:** Booking is in whole days, not by the hour or half day. Event spaces cannot be booked online yet.

## 💶 Surcharge per additional person

No more separate price for every number of people: a single room price now covers all occupancies.

* **One price for all occupancies:** For prices of type "Per night, per room", you enter a "Surcharge per additional person" in the "Room specific" section. €80 for 2 people and a €15 surcharge make €110 for 4 people. Previously every occupancy needed its own price, and if one was missing, the booking found no price.
* **"Up to" instead of "exactly":** A price with a surcharge applies to **up to** its number of people; only above that does each additional person, up to the number of beds, cost the surcharge per night – in the example, one person pays €80. A price for exactly the booked number of people takes precedence; prices without a surcharge still apply only to exactly their number of people. You set a minimum number of guests for online booking with "Min. occupancy" under **Settings → Online booking**.
* The surcharge appears under "Surcharges / discounts" on the reservation, the invoice and in online booking. Price rules and day prices change it in the same proportion as the room price; the price calendar, REST API and AI interface also take it into account.

## 🗓️ More flexible calendar export

* **Arrival and departure times:** In a room's export settings you can include saved arrival and departure times in the iCal calendar. If one of the two times is missing, it is filled in as 00:00; without times, and for room blocks, entries stay all-day.
* **Export period:** By default only current and future reservations and room blocks are exported, including stays in progress. With **"Entire calendar"**, past entries are included too.
* **Redesigned settings:** Export options and calendar addresses are neatly arranged, and you copy addresses with one click.

## ✉️ Emails and invoices

* **Booking confirmation with one click:** The "Confirmation" button in the reservation details opens the email directly for editing – rooms, recipient and PDF attachments such as confirmation and terms and conditions are already filled in. You define the email template and attachments under **Settings → General → Booking confirmation**; without an email template the button does not appear. With "Send right away" the email goes out directly without editing. Contribution by @MeisterAdebar ([#318](https://github.com/developeregrem/fewohbee/discussions/318)).
* **Send the online check-in invitation manually:** If an "Invitation template" is chosen under **Settings → Online check-in**, you send the invitation directly to the booker from the reservation's "Online check-in" tab, also again. The status line shows whether it has already been sent or whether an automation takes care of it. Contribution by @MeisterAdebar ([#318](https://github.com/developeregrem/fewohbee/discussions/318)).
* **Company details in online check-in:** Under **Settings → Online check-in** you can have company, VAT ID and a different invoice address requested. On confirmation, they end up in a business address of the main guest and therefore on their invoices.
* **Email for invoices:** On a contact address you can save a separate "Email for invoices", for example the accounting mailbox. New invoices are then sent to this address; existing ones remain unchanged. If you attach an invoice to an email from the reservation, the recipient switches accordingly, and a note shows this. Contribution by @MeisterAdebar ([#312](https://github.com/developeregrem/fewohbee/issues/312)).
* **Embedded templates:** Templates of type "General Email Template", for example a signature, can now be embedded in all email templates, and in language variants automatically in the matching language. Fixed: if a template was saved again in the visual editor or inserted by dragging, the link was lost, and emails and PDFs showed only the template's name (see "When upgrading").

## ✨ Further improvements

* **Room selection in online booking:** In the "Modern" design, guests choose the quantity per room and occupancy with "+" and "−". If only one selection fits the search, it is already filled in; otherwise a note above "Continue" says what is still missing.
* **Sorting by name:** Prices and extras appear sorted by name everywhere, in the same order.
* **Create calendar entries faster:** From the header of the reservation overview, the form only asks for calendar, title and time; you set the date and multiple days via the link below. Contribution by @MeisterAdebar ([#316](https://github.com/developeregrem/fewohbee/issues/316)).
* **Tidier popover:** In the popover of the reservation bars, phone numbers and email addresses no longer wrap. Invoice with status (click to open), contact details and times each have their own line with an icon. Contribution by @MeisterAdebar ([#314](https://github.com/developeregrem/fewohbee/issues/314)).
* **Holidays:** The federal state chosen under **Settings → Display settings for holidays** is now kept in the reservation overview even after reloading. Contribution by @MeisterAdebar ([#315](https://github.com/developeregrem/fewohbee/issues/315)).
* **Paging in the booking journal:** Arrows at the edge of the screen page through the pages and months, and in the overview and year view through the years; on tablets and smartphones also with a swipe. The arrows next to the month name are removed. "Add booking" is the round "+" button at the bottom right on touch devices. Contribution by @MeisterAdebar.
* **Smartphone polish:** Wide tables scroll sideways on their own instead of making the whole page wider. The buttons in the booking journal and for custom automations stay on screen, and "New automation" is a round "+" button on touch devices. Month and year selection is back in the tourism statistics. Contribution by @MeisterAdebar ([#327](https://github.com/developeregrem/fewohbee/issues/327)).
* **Code quality and stability:** Numerous internal clean-ups, bug fixes and additional automated tests make existing processes more robust.

## 🐞 Bug fixes

* **Calendar import across the clock change:** All-day entries spanning the clock change end on the correct day again; previously the last night was missing in spring, and conflicts arose in autumn. The next import corrects existing reservations as long as the entry is still in the feed. Contribution by @MeisterAdebar ([#318](https://github.com/developeregrem/fewohbee/discussions/318#discussioncomment-19265442)).
* **Online booking with a percentage discount:** If a discounted price fell exactly on half a cent (e.g. 25% off €44.50), the booking reported a changed price on every attempt and could not be completed.
* **Online check-in with ID requirement:** If another guest was entered or the prefilled name was overwritten, the form could be submitted without an ID number.
* **Calendar export with the same arrival and departure day:** Such reservations could be rejected by calendars. They now occupy the whole day.
* **Check receipts:** Receipt proposals could not be discarded, and after a booking of €0.00 or a negative amount, receipts from the same period could no longer be opened.
* **AI assistants:** Under **Settings → AI assistants**, accesses could not be revoked via the bin icon.

## ⚠️ When upgrading

* **Check calendar export:** After the update, existing calendars also export only current and future entries. If past entries should still be transferred, choose **"Entire calendar"** in the room's export settings.
* **Check embedded templates:** Check your existing templates using the preview. If only the name of an embedded template appears in its place, delete the entry and insert it again.
