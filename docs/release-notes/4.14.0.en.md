## 🗓️ More flexible calendar export

* **Arrival and departure times:** In a room's export settings you can include saved arrival and departure times in the iCal calendar. The option is switched off by default. If only one time is saved, the missing time is filled in as 00:00 in the export; without times, entries stay all-day. Room blocks also stay all-day.
* **Choose the export period:** By default only current and future reservations and room blocks are exported, including stays in progress and departures today. After the update this also applies to existing calendars. If you still want to transfer past entries, choose **"Entire calendar"** in the export settings.
* **Clearer settings:** Calendar synchronisation has been redesigned. Export options and calendar addresses are neatly arranged; public and private addresses can be copied directly with the copy icon.

## 🌍 Online booking and online check-in in several languages

* **Languages for guests:** Under **Settings → General → Guests** you define the languages in which online booking and online check-in can be offered.
* **Translate your own texts:** Translations can be added to the fields guests see: room categories, rooms, extras, guest categories, tourist tax, arrival notice and the texts of online booking and online check-in. If a translation is missing, the text appears in the installation's language.
* **Language of the booking page:** When opened directly, the booking page follows the browser language and offers a language selector. When embedded, the website sets the language via the `lang` parameter in the iFrame address, e.g. `…/book?embed=1&lang=en`; without the parameter it appears in the installation's language.
* **Salutations:** Salutations are maintained as a table with their version in each language. The salutation saved for the guest and used in conversations is always the one in the installation's language.
* **Emails and PDFs in the guest's language:** In the template list, templates can be given language variants. Automatic emails to the booker use the variant in their language, including attached PDFs such as terms and conditions or invoices; without a variant, the template itself is used. The language is remembered during online booking and online check-in and can be changed on the guest; the link to online check-in opens in that language. Notifications to you stay in the installation's language.
* **Amounts and countries:** Amounts are shown in the format of the guest's language. The country preselected during booking follows the browser.

## 💶 Surcharge per additional person

* **One price for all occupancies:** For prices of type "Per night, per room", you can now enter a "Surcharge per additional person" in the "Room specific" section, for all room categories from holiday apartment to seminar room. Previously, each room price applied only to exactly its number of people: you had to create a separate price for every occupancy (1 person, 2 people, 3 people, etc.), and if one was missing, the booking found no price. Now a single price is enough: €80 for 2 people and a €15 surcharge make €110 for 4 people.
* **"Up to" instead of "exactly":** A price with a surcharge applies to **up to** the number of people entered; only above that does each additional person, up to the number of beds, cost the surcharge per night. In the example, one person therefore pays €80. A price for exactly the booked number of people takes precedence, for instance a cheaper price for one person; prices without a surcharge still apply only to exactly their number of people. If a room should only be bookable online from a minimum number of guests, "Min. occupancy" under **Settings → Online booking** helps.
* The surcharge appears under "Surcharges / discounts" on the reservation, on the invoice and in online booking. Price rules and day prices change it in the same proportion as the room price. The price calendar, REST API and AI interface also take it into account.

## 🏛️ Event spaces

Seminar rooms, banquet halls, barns or outdoor areas can now be rented out properly: booked by the day, with their own prices and kept cleanly separate from overnight stays.

* **Finally bookable by the day:** Under **Settings → Room categories** you can choose the room type "Event space" for a category. Previously you had to set up a hall as a normal room and book a Saturday party as an overnight stay from Saturday to Sunday: in the occupancy plan it started at midday and reached into Sunday, and reservation and invoice spoke of nights. Now "To" is the last day, so "From Sat to Sat" is exactly one day. In the occupancy plan the event fills whole days, a click on the Saturday cell books the Saturday, and reservation and invoice refer to days and the last day.
* **One price for the whole group:** For event spaces the calculation types are called "Per day, per room" and "Per day, per person". Together with the new surcharge per additional person, a single price is enough instead of one price for every number of people: €500 for 50 people and a €5 surcharge make €550 for 60 people; a party of 30 people costs €500. A price applies either to accommodation or to event spaces.
* **No distorted statistics, no tourist tax:** Previously every guest at a party in the banquet hall counted as an overnight stay, the hall skewed the occupancy and with it the occupancy rules of dynamic pricing, and the invoice contained tourist tax that you had to delete by hand. Now event spaces are left out of occupancy, overnight and origin statistics, and no tourist tax is charged for them. Their revenue still counts.
* **Not bookable online by accident:** Event spaces appear neither in online booking nor in its room selection; you no longer have to deselect the hall there yourself.
* **Room changes with a safety net:** A reservation can only be moved to a room of the same room type, including by dragging in the occupancy plan. This way the party does not end up in the holiday apartment and the holiday guest does not end up in the seminar room.
* **Internally, the following day still counts:** An event is stored like an overnight stay up to the following day, so "Sat to Sat" becomes "Sat to Sun" internally. The app converts this everywhere. The following day only appears as the end date of a room block ("free again from this day"), in the reservation data of the REST API and AI interface, and in your own templates for emails, PDFs and invoices that output `endDate`. In templates, reservations and room items provide `visibleEndDate` for this: the last day for events, otherwise the departure day.
* **Limits:** Booking is in whole days; there are no hours or half days. Guests cannot yet book event spaces online.

## 🔧 Other changes

* **Code quality and stability:** Numerous internal clean-ups, targeted bug fixes and additional automated tests make existing processes more robust and help to catch errors early in future changes.
* **Room selection in online booking:** In the "Modern" design, guests choose the quantity per room and occupancy with "+" and "−", also on smartphones. If only one selection fits the search, for example with only one room category, it is already filled in. If the selection does not yet match the search, a note above "Continue" says what is missing.
* **Online check-in with company details:** Under **Settings → Online check-in** you can have "Company details for the invoice" requested. Guests then enter company, VAT ID and a different invoice address if needed. On confirmation, the details end up in a business address of the main guest and therefore on their invoices. The request is switched off by default.
* **Email for invoices:** A separate "Email for invoices" can be saved on a contact address, for example the accounting mailbox. New invoices take over this address; if it is empty, the normal email is used as before; existing invoices remain unchanged. If you attach an invoice when writing an email from the reservation, the recipient switches to that invoice's email, and a note below the field shows this. A recipient you entered yourself is not overwritten. Contribution by @MeisterAdebar ([#312](https://github.com/developeregrem/fewohbee/issues/312)).
* **Booking confirmation from the reservation:** The reservation details have a "Confirmation" button. It opens the familiar email editing view without the step through reservation and template selection: all rooms of the booking are selected, and the recipient and PDF attachments such as confirmation and terms and conditions are already filled in. You define which email template and which attachments these are under **Settings → General → Booking confirmation**; without an email template the button does not appear. If the booker has several email addresses, you choose the address at the button. With "Send right away" the email goes out directly without editing. Templates and attachments come in the booker's language if a variant exists. Contribution by @MeisterAdebar ([#318](https://github.com/developeregrem/fewohbee/discussions/318)).
* **Send the online check-in invitation manually:** If an "Invitation template" is chosen under **Settings → Online check-in**, the invitation can be sent directly to the booker from a reservation's "Online check-in" tab, also again. The status line shows whether it has already been sent or whether an automation takes care of it. Link and QR code are together in one menu. Contribution by @MeisterAdebar ([#318](https://github.com/developeregrem/fewohbee/discussions/318)).
* **Sorting by name:** Prices and extras appear sorted by name, always in the same order, in the price overview, in reservations and invoices, and in online booking and online check-in.
* **Create calendar entries faster:** When creating one from the header of the reservation overview, the form only asks for calendar, title and time. You set a different date or several days via the link below; the end time appears as soon as a time or an end date is entered. Contribution by @MeisterAdebar ([#316](https://github.com/developeregrem/fewohbee/issues/316)).
* **Popover of the reservation bars:** The popover in the reservation overview becomes as wide as its longest line, so phone numbers and email addresses no longer wrap in the middle of a line. The invoice status follows the invoice number, and a click on the number opens the invoice. Invoice, phone, mobile, email and times each have their own line with an icon, and identical numbers of several addresses appear only once. The popover shows the reservation status only as long as there is no invoice. Contribution by @MeisterAdebar ([#314](https://github.com/developeregrem/fewohbee/issues/314)).
* In the reservation overview, the federal state chosen under **Settings → Display settings for holidays** is now also kept after reloading the page. Contribution by @MeisterAdebar ([#315](https://github.com/developeregrem/fewohbee/issues/315)).
* **Paging in the booking journal:** Arrows at the left and right edge of the screen page through the month and then on to the next or previous month; going back takes you to the last page of the previous month. In the overview and the year view they page to the next or previous year. On tablets and smartphones this also works with a swipe left or right; wide tables first move sideways. The arrows next to the month name are removed. On touch devices, "Add booking" is the round "+" button at the bottom right, as in the reservation overview; in the overview it opens the "Add" menu with month and opening balance. Contribution by @MeisterAdebar.
* Fix: In the tourism statistics, the month and year selection with the paging arrows was missing on tablets and smartphones. It is back. Contribution by @MeisterAdebar ([#327](https://github.com/developeregrem/fewohbee/issues/327)).
* Fix: Wide tables, for example for invoices, guests and prices, can now be scrolled sideways on their own on smartphones instead of making the whole page wider. Contribution by @MeisterAdebar ([#327](https://github.com/developeregrem/fewohbee/issues/327)).
