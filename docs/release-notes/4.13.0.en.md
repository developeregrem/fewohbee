This update brings some big new features: guests check in online before they arrive, prices adapt to weekends, trade fairs and occupancy, and AI assistants can work directly in FewohBee. FewohBee is also much more pleasant to use on smartphones and tablets.

## 🛎️ Online check-in

Less paperwork on arrival: your guests enter their arrival time, registration form details and extras themselves beforehand.

* **One link for everything:** Send it via an automation (block "Online check-in link"), paste it into the chat of a booking portal or add it to a PDF template as a QR code. Guests first confirm arrival, departure and surname; after that the form is prefilled with the details already known – returning guests only need a quick check. After submitting, the same link shows an overview of the stay.
* **You stay in control:** Nothing reaches your guest data unchecked. In the reservation's "Check-in" tab you see for each person what changes, match them to an existing guest or create a new one, and confirm with one click. To match against guests who are not yet part of the reservation, the user needs access to guest data. Notes from the guest are added to the reservation remark; unconfirmed details are deleted 30 days after departure.
* **Sell extras along the way:** In the check-in, guests add extras such as breakfast for the whole stay and see the total price straight away. You choose in the price settings which prices are offered; extras already booked cannot be deselected.
* **Everything at a glance:** Pending check-ins appear in the notification window, the Frontdesk checklist shows who has already checked in, and the registration form can be printed straight from the tab. Guests who do not check in online can be marked as "Checked in at the desk".
* **Automations:** New are the triggers *Guest checked in online* and *Check-in completed (confirmed online or at the desk)* as well as the condition *State of the online check-in*. When you first switch the feature on, a sample automation (still disabled) is ready that sends the link 7 days before arrival – with a matching [email template](https://github.com/developeregrem/fewohbee-examples/blob/master/templates/email-online-checkin.txt) to start from.
* Everything is set up under **Settings → Online check-in**, including a preview of the guest page.
* Also: guests now have a **Nationality** field, and the "Guests" tab lets you enter all fellow travellers – including babies who do not count towards occupancy. The origin statistics include them; occupancy still only counts occupied beds.

## 💶 Dynamic pricing

The right price at the right time: FewohBee now adjusts your base prices automatically – and keeps what you promised your guests.

* **Price rules** change base prices by a percentage, e.g. at weekends, for a trade fair, at short notice, for early bookers or depending on occupancy. They apply to individual or all branches and room categories; limits and rounding are adjustable.
* The **price calendar** shows per branch, room category and number of people what a night costs and why. There you set day prices for individual nights or periods, which take precedence over the rules.
* **A promise is a promise:** The price is fixed on the reservation when it is booked; later changes to base prices do not change existing bookings. If it differs from the current prices, the reservation shows this and you decide whether it stays. Prices that still belong to a booking are only deactivated when deleted.
* Online bookings and bookings via AI assistants are only created at the price shown. If it has changed in the meantime, the new price is shown again for confirmation.
* Special periods can be given a name, such as that of the trade fair – it appears next to the period under Prices.
* For pricing tools and your own software: the REST API lets you set day prices and query availability, with a separate permission that grants no access to reservation data ([Day Prices endpoint](https://github.com/developeregrem/fewohbee/wiki/REST-API#day-prices)).

## 🤖 AI assistants (MCP)

Connect AI assistants such as Claude Code, Cursor or n8n directly to FewohBee via MCP (Model Context Protocol). They create analyses and operations reports, check availability and prices and – if you allow it – create reservations including extras. Every reservation made by an AI appears in the notification window.

* **Try it out:** Ask your AI to look for trade fairs, town festivals, concerts and school holidays in your area. It compares the dates with your occupancy and bookings so far, suggests suitable prices and, once you give the go-ahead, stores them as a price rule or day price. That way you never let rooms go too cheaply during a big event again. (Your AI tool needs internet access for this.)
* **Hand in receipts:** Give the AI an invoice or till receipt as a PDF or photo, and it hands in a booking proposal with accounts and tax rates. You review it under **Booking journal → Receipt proposals** – with matching payments and a hint about possible duplicates. Nothing is booked until you confirm. The "Hand in receipts" permission requires the Cash journal role and gives no access to bank statements or booking journal entries.
* **You decide what the AI may do:** You create the access in your profile under "API access" with the use "AI assistant (MCP)" and define what the AI may see, which personal data it receives and what it may change. Your guests' names, contact details and remarks only reach it with your explicit permission. An AI access does not work with the REST API and expires after one year at the latest.
* Programs in which you store the access as a token can be connected. Browser-based AI assistants that sign in via OAuth (e.g. claude.ai or ChatGPT) are not supported yet.
* The feature is off by default and is switched on under **Settings → AI assistants**; the interface requires HTTPS. Self-hosted installations first set `MCP_ENABLED=true` in the `.env`. Everything else is in the [wiki](https://github.com/developeregrem/fewohbee/wiki/MCP-AI-Assistant).

## 📱 On the go with Smartphone and Tablet

Reservations and the occupancy plan can now be used comfortably with your finger on smartphone and tablets too.

* **Full-screen reservations:** A reservation opens like a page of its own – large buttons, no cut-off tables, the action bar always within reach. Change the period, assign guests, create an invoice: all without zooming. The back gesture takes you exactly to where you were in the overview. The other dialogs open full screen too.
* **New reservation view on all devices:** The detail page has been redesigned – clearer and easier to use.
* **Week view:** The week icon next to the table and year views shows whole weeks starting on Monday with the calendar week, without menu and filters; the rooms use the full screen height. Browse with the arrow keys or a swipe.
* **Swipe and drag:** The normal table also pages sideways with a swipe; a swipe up hides the menu and filters, a swipe down brings them back. Mark arrival to departure with a long press and drag, and create new reservations with the round "+" button. On narrow screens the main menu opens as a sidebar.
* **Quick contact:** Phone numbers and email addresses in a reservation's popover are clickable; if the guest has several numbers, "Call" asks which one to use. On smartphones and tablets the first tap opens the popover, the second opens the reservation.
* Touch operation of the reservation overview is a contribution by @MeisterAdebar ([#311](https://github.com/developeregrem/fewohbee/pull/311)) – thank you!

## 🧾 Invoices and templates

* **Due date per invoice:** You set it when creating and editing an invoice, next to the invoice number and date. It is prefilled with the payment period from the invoice settings; via a menu you can choose your own number of days or the arrival or departure instead. The date applies to the PDF and the e-invoice alike and stays even if you change the period later. Existing invoices keep the date they have printed so far.
* New trigger for automations: **"X days after the invoice due date"** – also on the due date (0) or before it (negative values). Ideal for payment reminders.
* **Invoice template per payment method:** e.g. an A5 template for cash payments. Invoices with this payment method use it automatically, also via automation.
* **Custom fonts:** Install TTF and compatible OTF fonts directly in the template overview and then select them in PDF templates.
* **Tidier template editor:** The blocks stay visible while scrolling, the editor adapts to narrower windows, and pasting from the clipboard and moving by dragging work again. Email templates only offer what arrives reliably in emails: image upload and the payment QR code are reserved for PDF templates; your own images via a public link still work in code mode.

## ✨ More improvements

* **Turnover forecast:** The turnover statistics now also show the expected turnover from existing reservations – room price, extras and tourist tax in the month of departure, hatched above the invoiced turnover, excluding cancellations and conflicts ([#302](https://github.com/developeregrem/fewohbee/issues/302)).
* **Online booking:** A placeholder text in the comment field asks e.g. for the arrival time without making the form longer – under **Settings → Online booking → Checkout Texts** ([#299](https://github.com/developeregrem/fewohbee/issues/299)).
* **Bank statement import:** Unfinished imports are kept even after logging out and are deleted after 2 days without changes.

## ⚠️ When upgrading

* **Enter the public address:** Links for guests need the public address of your installation. Enter it under **Settings → General → Public address** (e.g. `https://fewohbee.meine-pension.de`) or set `PUBLIC_BASE_URI` in the `.env`.
* **Check concurrent bookings:** The "Multiple occupancy" setting is now called "Concurrent bookings allowed": guests from different bookings can then share a room during the same period. The feature and your settings stay the same – please check for all rooms anyway that it is set as intended.
* Update **fewohbee-dockerized** via `update-docker.sh` so that custom fonts can be installed.
* Bank statement imports that are open during the update need to be uploaded once more.
