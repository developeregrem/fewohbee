## 🔔 Notifications and release notes

* The new **notification window** in the navigation bar brings together calendar imports that need checking, today's reminders and online bookings. Conflicts appear in red, reminders in yellow, notices in blue. A click opens the familiar dialog; your access rights still apply, read-only access included.
* Online bookings stay visible until you dismiss them. The profile menu shows the installed version and, under **What's new**, all release notes. After an update a notice appears in the notification window.
* ⚠️ **When upgrading:** calendar reminders now live in the notification window. Their previous button in the reservation overview is gone; the button for imports that need checking stays.

## 🤖 Automations

* The new **Show a notification in the app** action is available for every trigger, for overdue invoices for instance. Under Settings → Automations you choose the importance (Info, Warning, Critical), who sees it (everyone or a single role) and optionally an explanatory text.
* For online bookings and calendar imports, app notifications are switched on already. The existing emails carry on; both channels can be switched off independently.
* **Scheduled automations** can run every day, Monday to Friday or Monday to Saturday from a chosen full hour. Excluded days are caught up on the next allowed day. Existing rules stay on every day from 00:00; the cron job still checks every quarter of an hour and runs them on the first matching pass.
* When sending or attaching an invoice you can pick a **PDF invoice template** — for attachments on template emails as well. Without a choice the default template applies.

## 🏷️ Portal fees per booking origin

* On a booking origin you record the **commission and payment fee as percentages**, along with who collects the payment and who collects the tourist tax. These details are written along when a reservation is created, so that a contract changed later does not retroactively alter what applies to older bookings. Contributed by @MeisterAdebar ([#293](https://github.com/developeregrem/fewohbee/pull/293)), see also the [documentation](https://www.fewohbee.app/en/documentation/manual-origins.html).
* The new **Create percentage booking entry** automation books the deduction into the booking journal — either with the rate from the origin or one you enter yourself. Debit and credit account, tax rate and remark are set on the automation; `%number%` is replaced by the invoice number.
* For invoice templates there are the **Booking origin, OTA commission and OTA payment fee** snippets, so these can be shown on the invoice itself as well.
* What the fees apply to is stated on the invoice positions. On the price, the **Part of a portal booking** switch decides what is sold at reception and therefore carries no fees; on a position added by hand this can be changed. A separately stated tourist tax stays commission-free, provided it is set up separately at the portal too.
* A booking entry can be marked as **waiting for a document number**. While it is missing, the month cannot be closed. Accounts and tax rates that an automation books with can no longer be deleted.

## 💳 Payment QR code and invoice templates

* The new **Payment QR code (GiroCode)** snippet carries the payee, IBAN, amount and invoice number into the banking app for a SEPA credit transfer. Size and position can be adjusted. It only appears when bank details are on file, the amount can be transferred and the currency is euro. [Guide and example](https://github.com/developeregrem/fewohbee/wiki/Templates-Invoice) · contributed by @MeisterAdebar ([#280](https://github.com/developeregrem/fewohbee/pull/280)).
* Invoice templates can work out the **payment due date** from the invoice date and the payment period. [Guide](https://github.com/developeregrem/fewohbee/wiki/Templates-Invoice#payment-due-date) · contributed by @MeisterAdebar ([#279](https://github.com/developeregrem/fewohbee/pull/279)).

## 🔐 Sign-in via single sign-on (OIDC)

* FewohBee supports central sign-in services such as Keycloak, Authentik, Entra ID, Google Workspace and Zitadel. Once it is set up through the `.env`, **Sign in with SSO** appears; without that configuration everything stays as it is. [Configuration](https://github.com/developeregrem/fewohbee/wiki/Konfiguration#single-sign-on-oidc).
* Users have to exist in FewohBee already. The first sign-in links the account permanently to the SSO account through the confirmed email address; later email changes do not undo the link. **You still manage roles in FewohBee.** If access is withdrawn at the sign-in service, no new sign-in is possible through it.
* The link is visible in the profile and can be removed by administrators. Password and passkey stay usable; password sign-in can optionally be switched off.
> Make sure to update **fewohbee-dockerized** via update-docker.sh as well.

## 🔌 REST API: prices and tourist tax

* New are a **price list, a rate calendar per occupancy and a price quote for a stay**. Like the invoice, the calculation takes season periods, weekdays, minimum stay and reductions for children into account — for prices on your website, for instance.
* For the **tourist tax**, the settings and the monthly reports can be retrieved. Existing queries are unchanged.
* The new **Read prices** and **Read tourist tax** permissions are not granted to existing API tokens automatically. Create a new token for them or replace the existing one. 📖 [All queries and examples](https://github.com/developeregrem/fewohbee/wiki/REST-API).

## 🕒 Opening, check-in and check-out times

* Under Settings → Branches you record **opening hours** with up to two time ranges per weekday and a free-text note. Days without times count as closed. Contributed by @MeisterAdebar ([#284](https://github.com/developeregrem/fewohbee/pull/284)).
* Added to that are a **check-in window**, the latest **check-out time** and a note for arrangements. Without an end to the check-in window, **Check-in from …** applies.
* Both are available as the **Opening hours of the branch** and **Check-in times of the branch** snippets in the template editor, and as master data in the REST API. The new **Read branches** API permission is not granted to existing tokens automatically.
* The times are for information. They change neither availability and bookings nor the individual arrival time of a reservation, and are not entered there automatically.

## 🗓️ Calendar imports

* Before setting up an import you can check the entries and choose which ones are taken over as reservations. Unwanted and similar labels can be excluded; if you wish, the same rules apply to all room imports from one portal. Reservations already imported stay unchanged. [Check the calendar before importing](https://www.fewohbee.app/en/documentation/manual-rooms.html).
* Your own calendars now support **recurring entries with RRULE** on import. Contributed by @MeisterAdebar ([#278](https://github.com/developeregrem/fewohbee/pull/278)).

## 🛏️ More flexible booking rules

* Under **Settings → Online Booking → Booking rules**, freely chosen weekdays, minimum nights and room categories replace the fixed split into weekday and weekend.
* With **Minimum stay on arrival** the day of arrival decides. With **Minimum stay for a night** the whole stay has to be long enough as soon as it covers one of the chosen nights. On top of that you can close individual weekdays **to arrival or departure**.
* **Special periods** support every kind of rule and replace the general minimum stay within their date range, including with lower values.
* A **calendar per room category** shows the minimum nights actually required for each arrival day. Additional restrictions caused by other days are marked and explained in the tooltip.

### ⚠️ Please check after the update: Sunday now counts as a weekday

Sunday arrivals take the **weekday value** from now on: Mon–Thu / Fri–Sun becomes Sun–Thu / Fri–Sat. With 4 nights on weekdays and 2 at the weekend so far, Sunday now requires 4 nights ([#286](https://github.com/developeregrem/fewohbee/issues/286)). Without a weekday value, the previous weekend value is kept. **Check the new calendar and adjust the rules where needed.**

## 🔧 Further changes

* **Prices for several room categories:** existing assignments are kept; other prices without a category still apply to all. The REST API adds `roomCategories`; `roomCategory` remains for now and is only filled when there is exactly one category.
* **Import rules in the booking journal** can be edited under Bank import → Settings, without deleting them and creating them again.
* **Invalid or unusually long reservation periods** lead to a plain message in the reservation dialog on availability checks and price calculation, instead of a server error.
* Snippets in the template editor can show additional hints on hover.
* The daily cleanup task removes old notifications with the same retention period as the logs (90 days by default).
